How it works

  1. Save. Click the PromptVault button on any AI chat, or right-click selected text anywhere.
  2. Organize. Give it a title, tags, and keep the source link — all in one place.
  3. Reuse. Search your library and copy the perfect prompt whenever you need it.

🔒 Private by design

PromptVault collects nothing. Your prompts live only in your browser via chrome.storage.local and never leave your device unless you export them. Read the full privacy policy.
